This document lists capabilities available today for clinic teams and patients, plus features still planned.

1. Current features

1.1 Dietitian / Clinic

Practice overview

  • Dashboard with clients, tasks, appointments, invoices, messages, and clients needing attention
  • Recent activity overview

Client management

  • Client roster with search, filters, tags, create, and archive
  • Join codes so patients can connect themselves
  • Personal clinical chart: identity plus a default clinical profile (visit context, lifestyle, health history, eating patterns, nutrition profile, pregnancy notes)
  • Chart sidebar: dated clinical notes, meal notes, eating habits, goals, documents (PDF / Word / TXT), and tags
  • Custom forms stay separate from the default clinical profile (build, assign, review in-progress and submitted)
  • Measurement tracking and evolution (weight, height, BMI, waist, hips, body fat, muscle mass, skinfolds, labs)
  • Review patient daily tracking with labeled amounts and energy; open full nutrition facts for a logged food
  • Care timeline
  • Portal connection status, invites, and disconnect handling

Meal planning & nutrition

  • Multi-day / multi-week meal plans with publish to the patient portal
  • Meal / recipe library
  • Food database (catalog + clinic custom foods)
  • In-plan food search with quantity, unit, filters, and nutrition columns
  • Nutrition facts popup (macros and micros) from search or a logged food
  • Automatic nutrition calculations
  • Habit library and habit assignment

Patient evaluations

  • Custom form builder on the client chart
  • Assign forms to patients without mixing them into the default clinical profile
  • Review in-progress and submitted answers

Calendar & appointments

  • Day / week / month calendar
  • Schedule, cancel, and reschedule workflows
  • Patient visit requests from the portal
  • Patient reschedule and cancellation requests from the portal

Messaging & notifications

  • Secure 1:1 realtime messaging with patients
  • In-app notifications with unread badges

Documents

  • Upload and manage client files from the chart (PDF, Word, TXT on Personal data)
  • Share with the patient or keep internal to the clinic

Billing & tasks

  • Quotations and invoices: draft, issue, send, mark paid, overdue, cancel
  • Document preview and printable / save-as-PDF
  • Clinic task list with priorities and due dates

Analytics

  • Practice analytics: activity, financial summary, clients needing attention

Clinic settings

  • Practice profile, timezone, locale, currency, units
  • Appointment defaults and invoice defaults

Plan add-ons

  • AI assistance (summaries, meal-plan help, notes, message drafts) on plans that include it
  • Workflow automations (reminders, messages, tasks) on plans that include them

1.2 Patient / Client portal

Home

  • Personal dashboard with next appointment, today’s tracking, meal plan snapshot, and messages
  • Live pending-forms count on Forms
  • Notifications from the header / sidebar bell

My Plan

  • View published meal plan by week and day
  • See meals, foods/recipes, and nutrition totals
  • One-tap log of planned meals into the daily log

Daily log

  • Add food on the same page (search, filters, quantity, unit, nutrition columns) — same picker as the clinic
  • Open full nutrition facts for a food
  • Water, exercise, sleep, and habit tracking with labeled values
  • Quick weight log
  • Browse any past date

Progress

  • Today’s tracking overview
  • Measurement evolution charts (weight, height, BMI, body composition, and more)

Assessments

  • Complete custom forms assigned by the dietitian (separate from clinic chart notes)
  • Save progress and submit when ready
  • Forms badge updates as soon as a form is assigned or submitted

Messages

  • Chat with the dietitian in realtime

Appointments

  • View upcoming and past appointments
  • Request a new visit
  • Request reschedule or cancellation

Documents

  • View files shared by the clinic
  • Upload files, download, and delete own uploads

Billing

  • View invoices and quotations in a document preview
  • Print or save as PDF
  • Online payment is not built in

Profile & access

  • Edit personal details and emergency contact
  • Set energy and weight units
  • View clinic notes shared for allergies, preferences, and lifestyle context
  • Connect to multiple clinics and switch between them
  • Join another clinic with a code
  • Request to leave a clinic

2. Future features

Templates library

  • Meal plan templates to save and reuse full plan structures across clients
  • Recommendations templates for reusable guidance
  • Foods-to-avoid templates by condition
  • Clinic-owned templates plus system starter templates
  • Search and filter templates for faster plan building

Richer nutrition facts & charts

  • More detailed nutrition charts beyond basic macros
  • Visual breakdowns by meal, day, and plan period
  • Stronger progress charts and trend insights

AI assistant

  • Always-available in-product assistant in the dietitian workspace
  • Context-aware help while reviewing clients, building plans, and writing notes
  • Suggestions for recommendations, foods to avoid, and plan adjustments
  • Draft support reviewed by the dietitian before sharing

Product improvements

  • Faster meal-plan building with templates
  • Stronger clinical recommendations workflow
  • Better reporting and visual analytics
  • Continuous workflow and usability improvements based on clinic feedback

Feature requests

We welcome new feature requests that are realistic for a nutrition practice platform. Please share your priorities and use cases. We will review feasibility, clinical value, and delivery timeline with you.
