Product overview

Feature Overview

A complete overview of the CTN Solution Dietitian System capabilities for dietitians and their patients, plus the next features planned for delivery.

Document date: 23 August 2026 Version: v1 Prepared for: Client review

1. Current features

Capabilities available today for clinic teams and patients.

1.1 Dietitian / Clinic

Practice overview

  • Dashboard with clients, tasks, appointments, invoices, messages, and clients needing attention
  • Recent activity overview

Client management

  • Client roster with search, filters, tags, create, and archive
  • Join codes so patients can connect themselves
  • Full client chart: profile, goals, preferences, allergies, notes
  • Measurement tracking and evolution (weight, height, BMI, waist, hips, body fat, muscle mass)
  • Review patient daily tracking (food, water, exercise, sleep, habits)
  • Care timeline
  • Portal connection status, invites, and disconnect handling

Meal planning & nutrition

  • Multi-day / multi-week meal plans with publish to the patient portal
  • Meal / recipe library
  • Food database (catalog + clinic custom foods)
  • Automatic nutrition calculations
  • Habit library and habit assignment

Patient evaluations

  • Custom evaluation / assessment form builder
  • Assign forms to patients
  • Review in-progress and submitted answers

Calendar & appointments

  • Day / week / month calendar
  • Schedule, cancel, and reschedule workflows
  • Patient reschedule and cancellation requests from the portal

Messaging & notifications

  • Secure 1:1 realtime messaging with patients
  • In-app notifications with unread badges

Documents

  • Upload and manage client files
  • Share with patient or keep internal to the clinic

Billing & tasks

  • Invoices: draft, issue, send, mark paid, overdue, cancel
  • Printable invoice PDF
  • Clinic task list with priorities and due dates

Analytics

  • Practice analytics: activity, financial summary, clients needing attention

Clinic settings

  • Practice profile, timezone, locale, currency, units
  • Appointment defaults and invoice defaults

1.2 Patient / Client portal

Home

  • Personal dashboard with next appointment, today’s tracking, meal plan snapshot, messages, and notifications

My Plan

  • View published meal plan by week and day
  • See meals, foods/recipes, and nutrition totals
  • One-tap log of planned meals into the daily log

Daily log

  • Log food (search catalog + clinic foods, portions, meals)
  • Water, exercise, sleep, and habits tracking
  • Quick weight log
  • Browse any past date

Progress

  • Today’s tracking overview
  • Measurement evolution charts (weight, height, BMI, body composition, and more)

Assessments

  • Complete evaluations assigned by the dietitian
  • Save progress and submit when ready

Messages

  • Chat with the dietitian in realtime

Appointments

  • View upcoming and past appointments
  • Request reschedule or cancellation

Documents

  • View files shared by the clinic
  • Upload files, download, and delete own uploads

Billing

  • View invoices and open printable PDF

Profile & access

  • Edit personal details
  • View clinic notes (allergies, preferences, lifestyle)
  • Connect to multiple clinics and switch between them
  • Join another clinic with a code
  • Request to leave a clinic

2. Future features

Planned enhancements to strengthen clinical workflows, nutrition insight, and day-to-day productivity.

Templates library

  • Meal plan templates to save and reuse full plan structures across clients
  • Recommendations templates for reusable guidance (for example healthy lifestyle or condition-specific advice)
  • Foods to avoid templates by condition (for example diabetes, hypertension, high cholesterol, obesity)
  • Clinic-owned templates plus system starter templates
  • Search and filter templates for faster plan building

Richer nutrition facts & charts

  • More detailed nutrition charts beyond basic macros (for example fiber, sugars, sodium, and micronutrients when available)
  • Visual breakdowns by meal, day, and plan period
  • Clearer comparison views for foods, recipes, and meal plans
  • Stronger progress charts and trend insights for dietitians and patients

AI assistant

  • Always-available in-product AI assistant in the dietitian workspace
  • Context-aware help while reviewing clients, building plans, and writing notes or messages
  • Suggestions for recommendations, foods to avoid, and plan adjustments based on client profile and goals
  • Draft support for summaries, meal-plan ideas, consultation notes, and message replies (reviewed by the dietitian before sharing)

Product improvements

  • Faster meal-plan building with templates
  • Stronger clinical recommendations workflow (apply template, then customize per client)
  • Better reporting and visual analytics across nutrition and progress data
  • UX polish on charts, templates, and assistant for daily clinic use
  • Continuous workflow and usability improvements based on clinic feedback
Feature requests

We welcome new feature requests that are realistic for a nutrition practice platform. Please share your priorities and use cases. We will review feasibility, clinical value, and delivery timeline with you.
